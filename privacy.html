Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes how information submitted through the website's enquiry conversations and community form is handled. The website operator is responsible for deciding how requests are reviewed and how long records are retained.

Information collected

Depending on what you choose to submit, the website may collect your name, email address, country, preferred language, selected enquiry category, event or fan-meeting reference, conversation messages, and community request details. A random browser session identifier is stored locally so you can return to the same conversation during your browser session.

How information is used

Information is used to receive and respond to enquiries, review community requests, provide management follow-up when requested, maintain the service, and protect the website from misuse. Please do not submit passwords, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

Storage and access

Enquiries, messages and community requests are stored in the connected Supabase project. Access to private records is restricted to authorised management accounts and server-side operations. Public pages receive only published website content. The website does not install advertising trackers as part of this build.

Service providers

The website uses Supabase for database, authentication, storage and application services, and Vercel or another configured host for delivery of the website. Those providers process information as needed to operate the service under their own terms and privacy documents.

Retention and your choices

You may choose not to send an enquiry or community request. Records are retained by the operator for as long as they are needed to handle the request, maintain records and meet applicable obligations. The operator should apply a documented retention and deletion schedule. To request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, use the website's general enquiry route and identify that your request concerns privacy.

Security

Access to management tools is restricted through authenticated accounts and an authorised-user list. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your private conversation link or browser session with another person.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's operation or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
